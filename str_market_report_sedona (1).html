Summary

Sedona's short-term rental market runs on hiking season, not just weather. Demand peaks in spring and fall when trail conditions are ideal, and drops hard in mid-summer once daytime heat pushes outdoor visitors elsewhere. A well-positioned 3BR home with a private pool and easy trailhead access is set up to beat the comp set average below.

Why Comp D was excluded

Its occupancy and revenue sit well outside the range of the other five comps, and its listing lacks a pool — consistent with the shortfall. Keeping it in the average would understate what a comparably-amenitized property actually earns in this market.

Amenity recommendations

  • Private pool — present in 4 of 6 top performers and the strongest single driver of rate premium once summer heat sets in.
  • Trailhead proximity — the market's real seasonality driver. Peak season tracks hiking conditions, not just temperature.
  • Dedicated workspace — a smaller but real edge with shoulder-season remote-work travelers.

Data quality checks performed

  • Comp D flagged and excluded from all averages — occupancy and revenue fell well outside the comp range, consistent with its lack of a pool.
  • Every RevPAR figure computed as Occupancy × ADR, then cross-checked against each comp's reported annual revenue for internal consistency.
  • All comps drawn from the same trailing 12-month window, so no season is compared against a different year's data.
